A Letter To Every Student

"Teaching is not my side hustle. It is my deepest passion, my obsession, and my life's highest calling."

Hello everyone, I am Satyam Thakur. Right now, I am pursuing my B.Sc. from IIT Patna. But before any degree, title, or credential, I am a teacher at heart. Nothing brings me greater purpose than breaking down a complex, intimidating concept until the spark of clarity lights up in a student's eyes.

I did not reach here as a "born genius". I have sat on the back benches. I have known the cold anxiety of staring at a blackboard feeling completely blank, intimidated by peers who answered in seconds. I have made mistakes, faced self-doubt, and felt the heavy, suffocating weight of being labeled "just an average student".

The "Bade Bhaiya" Mentorship Pledge

To be completely transparent with you: even today, I am not flawless. I am still learning every single day from my own missteps. And that is the exact superpower I want to pass on to you. I don't want you to fear mistakes; I want to teach you how to analyze every misstep so you never repeat it again.

Mendorbit will never treat you like a customer or an enrollment roll number. We stand with you like an elder brother (एक बड़े भैया की तरह) — holding your hand through your hardest days, rebuilding your conceptual foundation from ground zero, until you stand with pride on the toppers list.

Big coaching corporations love to handpick students who already score 95% and plaster their faces on hoardings. My mission is the exact opposite: I want the student who scores 50%, who feels left behind, who feels hesitant to ask a basic doubt. We will rebuild your foundation brick by brick until you crack your exam with absolute mastery.

The Problem We Are Solving: The Digital Chaos Trap

Students today don't lack intelligence or ambition. They are trapped in an algorithmic battlefield engineered to steal their focus.

Physical vs. Virtual Environment

A student can easily shut the door of their physical room, turn off the television, and sit at their study desk. Physical environment badalna aasan hai.

However, the moment they open YouTube or social media to study, they are bombarded by recommendations, 30-second dopamine reels, clickbait drama, and endless notifications. Their virtual environment is fractured and chaotic.

Result: A planned 45-minute study session turns into 2 hours of guilt-ridden scrolling. Focus evaporates, and self-confidence shatters.

The Mendorbit Virtual Room

Mendorbit doesn't just dump video links on you. We provide a dedicated, distraction-free virtual study room where your mind can finally find stillness, structure, and deep concentration.

  • Zero Algorithm Distractions: No shorts, no algorithmic feeds, zero clickbait suggestions — only pure syllabus clarity.
  • Productivity Superchargers: Chapter progression trackers, handwritten notes, DPPs, and revision formula sheets.
  • Arranged Like a ₹15,000 Paid Batch: Disciplined lecture schedules, doubt clearing, and continuous assessment — 100% free.
"You bring the willingness to learn. We build the distraction-free virtual room where you can truly excel."
