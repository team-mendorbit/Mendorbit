Core Formulas at a Glance Chapter 1 Cheat Sheet
  • • Product of Two Numbers: HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b
  • • Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every composite number can be uniquely expressed as product of primes.
  • • Rational Number: Expressible as p/q (where q ≠ 0 and p, q are co-prime integers).
  • • Irrational Proof: If prime number p divides a², then p also divides a.

1. Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (Ankaganit ka Aadharbhoot Pramey)

CBSE board ke latest syllabus me Euclid's Division Lemma hata diya gaya hai. Ab Chapter 1 ki shuruaat Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic se hoti hai:

"Pratyek bhaiya sankhya (composite number) ko abhaiya sankhyaon (prime numbers) ke gunanphal ke roop me vyakt kiya ja sakta hai, aur ye gunankhandan advitiya (unique) hota hai."

2. How to Prove √2, √3 or √5 is Irrational (Guaranteed 3 Marks)

Board exam me har saal Method of Contradiction (Virodhabhas Vidhi) se irrationality prove karne ka ek prashn pakka pucha jata hai.

Step 1: Maan lijiye √5 ek rational number hai. Toh √5 = a/b (jahan a aur b co-prime integers hain aur b ≠ 0).

Step 2: Dono taraf square karne par: 5 = a² / b² => a² = 5b².

Step 3: Iska matlab 5, a² ko divide karta hai. Pramey ke anusaar, 5 number 'a' ko bhi divide karega. (Maan lijiye a = 5c).

Step 4: a ki value rakhne par: (5c)² = 5b² => 25c² = 5b² => b² = 5c².

Step 5: Iska matlab 5 number 'b' ko bhi divide karta hai. Lekin humne maana tha ki a aur b co-prime hain! Ye virodhabhas (contradiction) paida karta hai. Atah √5 ek apalmeen (irrational) sankhya hai. [Hence Proved]
