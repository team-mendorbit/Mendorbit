Top 10 NLUs in India: Rank-wise Cutoffs, Placement Stats & Real Campus Reviews

NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, and WBNUJS Kolkata detailed comparison with Category-wise opening & closing ranks and corporate package stats.

Securing admission to a prestigious National Law University (NLU) is the ultimate ambition for over 70,000 aspirants attempting the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) each year. However, selecting your NLU preferences during counseling requires deep clarity: Tier-1 universities excel in Tier-1 corporate law firm hires, while certain Tier-2 NLUs boast superior judicial service records and civil service mentorship.

In this authoritative guide, our Mendorbit academic research team breaks down the Top 10 National Law Universities in India based on NIRF law rankings, verified CLAT closing ranks, audited median placement packages, and campus mooting ecosystems.

1. Tier-1 NLUs: The Golden Trinity (AIR 1 - 350)

1. NLSIU Bengaluru (National Law School of India University)

NIRF Rank 1

CLAT General Cutoff: All India Rank 1 – 114 | Median CTC: ₹18.0 - ₹22.5 LPA

Universally regarded as the benchmark of legal education in Asia. NLSIU pioneered the 5-year integrated B.A. LL.B. curriculum. Top national corporate law firms—including Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas, Trilegal, Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, and AZB & Partners—recruit almost 80% of eligible graduates through Day Zero placements.

2. NALSAR University of Law, Hyderabad

NIRF Rank 3

CLAT General Cutoff: All India Rank 115 – 180 | Median CTC: ₹16.5 - ₹19.0 LPA

Famous for its liberal academic environment, flexible elective structures, and stellar international mooting teams (including Jessup and Willem C. Vis). Strong alumni footprint across London and Singapore corporate chambers, commercial arbitration, and the Supreme Court Bar.

3. WBNUJS Kolkata (West Bengal National University of Juridical Sciences)

NIRF Rank 4

CLAT General Cutoff: All India Rank 181 – 275 | Median CTC: ₹16.0 - ₹18.5 LPA

A powerhouse for corporate transaction law, financial derivatives, and competition law advisory. NUJS students consistently secure foreign training contracts (FTCs) with Magic Circle firms like Linklaters, Allen & Overy, and Clifford Chance.

2. Tier-2 NLUs: Competitive Powerhouses (AIR 350 - 1,200)

4. NLUJ Jodhpur (National Law University, Jodhpur)

NIRF Rank 5

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 276 – 400 | Median CTC: ₹14.5 - ₹17.0 LPA

Widely recognized for its rigorous corporate governance, IP law, and trade law scholarship. Offers specialized honors in Corporate Law and IPR. Placements rival Tier-1 universities consistently.

5. GNLU Gandhinagar (Gujarat National Law University)

NIRF Rank 7

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 401 – 550 | Median CTC: ₹13.0 - ₹15.5 LPA

Boasts India's largest integrated campus offering five diverse streams (B.A., B.Com., B.Sc., B.B.A., and B.S.W. LL.B.). High placement rate in PSUs (SEBI, ONGC, IOCL) and premier litigation teams in Delhi and Mumbai.

6. NLIU Bhopal (National Law Institute University)

NIRF Rank 8

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 551 – 720 | Median CTC: ₹12.0 - ₹14.5 LPA

Unrivaled reputation in Cyber Law, Intellectual Property, and Criminal Justice. Excellent state judicial services output, producing numerous judicial magistrates and high court advocates annually.

3. Prominent Metro & Emerging NLUs (Rank 7 to 10)

7. MNLU Mumbai (Maharashtra National Law University)

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 580 – 760 | Median CTC: ₹12.0 - ₹14.0 LPA

Strategic geographic advantage being in the financial capital of India. Massive year-round internship exposure at the Bombay High Court, NCLT, and headquarters of major banks and investment funds.

8. RGNUL Punjab, Patiala

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 750 – 1,150 | Median CTC: ₹9.0 - ₹11.5 LPA

World-class infrastructure, high-speed research facilities, and a dedicated Civil Services Examination (UPSC) coaching support network for students.

9. CNLU Patna (Chanakya National Law University)

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 1,000 – 1,450 | Median CTC: ₹8.5 - ₹10.5 LPA

Celebrated as one of the most prolific hubs for state judiciary qualifiers. Over 30% of eligible graduates qualify for PCS-J (Judicial Services) examinations in Bihar, UP, and Delhi.

10. NUALS Kochi (National University of Advanced Legal Studies)

CLAT General Cutoff: AIR 1,100 – 1,500 | Median CTC: ₹8.0 - ₹10.0 LPA

Top destination for Maritime Law, Environmental Law, and Intellectual Property Studies. Excellent student-to-faculty ratio and strong legal clinic activities.

5. How to Fill Your NLU Preferences for Maximum ROI

When preparing your counseling list on the CLAT Consortium portal, follow this battle-tested framework:

  • If Corporate Law is your sole goal: Put NLSIU, NALSAR, NUJS, NLUJ, and MNLU Mumbai at the top of your list. Proximity to corporate headquarters and alumni networks will pay massive dividends.
  • If Judicial Services (Judge/Magistrate) or UPSC is your goal: NLIU Bhopal, CNLU Patna, and RGNUL Patiala offer more supportive academic calendars and extensive peer groups preparing for civil services.
  • Never drop preference purely due to distance: The brand value of a Tier-1 NLU follows you throughout your 40-year career. A seat at NLSIU Bengaluru or NALSAR Hyderabad is worth traveling across the country for.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (CLAT Counseling FAQs)

Q: What score is needed in CLAT to get into the Top 3 NLUs?

Historically, a score of 95+ out of 120 marks in CLAT is typically required for General category candidates to secure an All India Rank under 250.

Q: Does National Law University Delhi (NLU Delhi) take CLAT scores?

No, NLU Delhi conducts its own separate entrance examination called AILET (All India Law Entrance Test) and does not participate in CLAT counseling.
