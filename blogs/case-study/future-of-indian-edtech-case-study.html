The Rise, Fall & Future of Online EdTech in India: Deep Dive Case Study

How bloated marketing budgets collapsed early unicorns, and why sustainable, teacher-first models like Mendorbit are winning student trust.

Between 2020 and 2022, India's EdTech ecosystem witnessed astronomical venture capital investment. Flashy celebrity endorsements, aggressive sales tactics, and sky-high valuations dominated headlines. Yet by 2024, multiple high-profile unicorns crumbled under the weight of customer acquisition costs and falling renewal rates.

1. Why The First Generation of EdTech Failed Students

The fundamental flaw was treating education as an aggressive software-as-a-service (SaaS) business driven by loan-based multi-year subscriptions rather than genuine pedagogical outcomes:

  • Outrageous CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): Spending up to 60% of revenue purely on marketing and television advertisements instead of educator compensation.
  • High-Pressure Loan Tactics: Forcing low-income parents into high-interest NBFC loans without clear refund policies.
  • One-Size-Fits-All Recorded Libraries: Neglecting individual student doubts, step-by-step hand-holding, and emotional motivation.
