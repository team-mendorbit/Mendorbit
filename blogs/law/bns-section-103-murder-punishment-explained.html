BNS Section 103 Explained: Murder Definition & Punishment under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita vs IPC 302

1 July 2024 se Bharat me naye criminal laws laagu ho chuke hain. Janiye purane IPC Section 302 ki jagah ab BNS Section 103 me murder aur mob lynching par kya naye kanoon aur sajaayein tay ki gayi hain.

1. IPC 302 se BNS 103 tak ka Safar: Kanoon me kya badla?

Pichle 160+ saalo se Bharat me hatya (Murder) ke mamle me jo sabse mashhoor dhara chal rahi thi, wo thi Indian Penal Code (IPC) ki Dhara 302. Lekin Parliament dwara naye kanoon pass karne ke baad Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023 ne IPC ko replace kar diya hai.

Ab kisi bhi murder ke FIR me IPC 302 nahi, balki seedhe BNS Section 103 charge sheet ki jati hai.

Important Legal Fact:

Jo aparadh 1 July 2024 se pehle hue hain, unki sunwai abhi bhi IPC ke tahat hi chalegi. 1 July 2024 ya uske baad hone wale sabhi crimes par BNS lagu hota hai.

2. BNS Section 103(1) me Murder ki Definition & Punishment

Section 103 ke Clause (1) ke anusaar, jo koi bhi vyakti murder karega, use do me se kisi ek saja ka samna karna hoga:

  • Death Penalty (Mrityudand / Fansi): Rarest of rare cases me.
  • Imprisonment for Life (Umrqaid): Jo aamtaur par ajeevan karavas hota hai.
  • Fine (Arthdand): Saja ke sath jurmana lagana bhi anivarya hai.

3. Section 103(2): Mob Lynching par Bharat ka Pehla Khas Kanoon

IPC me Mob Lynching (bheed dwara kisi ko maar dena) ka koi alag se section nahi tha. BNS Section 103(2) ne is kami ko pura kiya hai.

Section 103(2) kya kehta hai?

Jab 5 ya 5 se adhik vyakti milkar jaati (caste), samuday (community), ling (gender), bhasha ya dharamik aadhar par kisi vyakti ki hatya karte hain, toh bheed ke har ek sadasya ko murder ka doshi maana jayega aur unhe kam se kam 7 saal ki saja, umrqaid ya fansi di ja sakti hai.

5. CLAT, Judiciary & Law Aspirants ke liye Important Points

Agar aap CLAT 2026, Judiciary ya kisi law exam ki taiyari kar rahe hain, toh yaad rakhein ki BNS me Culpable Homicide ko Section 100 me define kiya gaya hai, jabki Murder ko Section 101 me aur uski Punishment ko Section 103 me rakha gaya hai.
