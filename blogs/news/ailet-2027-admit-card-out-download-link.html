AILET 2027 Admit Card Out: Direct Download Link, Steps & Exam Guidelines

The National Law University (NLU), Delhi has officially released the AILET 2027 Admit Card for candidates appearing for the All India Law Entrance Test (AILET) 2027. With the exam scheduled for 13 December 2026 (Sunday), downloading your Admit Card early and verifying every detail is crucial to avoid last-minute technical or center-related issues.

1. AILET 2027 Important Dates & Overview

Registered candidates appearing for B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), LL.M., or Ph.D. admissions at National Law University, Delhi can review all verified AILET 2027 schedule and portal parameters below:

Particular Official Details
Conducting Body National Law University (NLU), Delhi
Exam Name All India Law Entrance Test (AILET) 2027
Admit Card Release Date 24 November 2026 (Released)
Last Date to Download Admit Card 6 December 2026 (up to 1:30 PM)
AILET 2027 Exam Date 13 December 2026 (Sunday)
Exam Timing 02:00 PM to 04:00 PM (2 Hours)
Exam Mode Offline (Pen-and-Paper Test)
Official Website nationallawuniversitydelhi.in

Important Note: NLU Delhi will not send admit cards via post or courier. You must download it online before 6 December 2026 (1:30 PM) and carry a clear printed hard copy to the test center.

2. How to Download AILET 2027 Admit Card

Follow these simple steps to download your AILET 2027 Admit Card from the official NLU Delhi admission portal:

1
Visit the Official NLU Delhi Portal: Go to nationallawuniversitydelhi.in in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
2
Open Candidate Login: Click on the "AILET 2027 Candidate Login" section on the admission homepage.
3
Enter Login Credentials: Input your registered Username / Registration ID and Password carefully.
4
Download Admit Card PDF: Click on the "Download Admit Card" button visible on your candidate dashboard.
5
Verify & Print 2 Copies: Verify your Name, Roll Number, Photo, Signature, Exam Date, Reporting Time, and Center Address, then take 2 clear printouts on clean A4 paper for safety.

3. Forgot Your AILET Portal Password?

If you cannot remember your login password, reset it immediately on the NLU Delhi portal before the 6 December deadline:

Click on "Forgot Password" right below the Candidate Login fields on nationallawuniversitydelhi.in.

Enter your registered Email ID / Mobile Number or Application Number as prompted.

Verify the OTP / reset link sent to your email or phone, create a new password, and download your Admit Card.

4. Details to Verify on Your AILET 2027 Admit Card

Carefully inspect every printed field as soon as you open the PDF. If you spot any mismatch, contact NLU Delhi support well before exam day:

Candidate's Name & Roll Number
Application / Registration ID
Clear Photograph & Signature
Program (B.A. LL.B. / LL.M. / Ph.D.)
Exam Date (13 Dec) & Reporting Time
Exact Exam Center Name & Address

5. Mandatory Documents to Carry on Exam Day

Entry to the examination hall will be strictly prohibited without these documents. Keep your exam kit packed the night before:

Printed Copy of AILET 2027 Admit Card (Digital copies on mobile phones are not accepted)
Original Aadhaar Card / E-Aadhaar
OR Original PAN Card / Voter ID
OR Original Passport / Driving License
Two Black/Blue Ballpoint Pens & 2 Photos

6. Key Exam Day Guidelines for AILET 2027

Reach Early: Arrive at your allotted test center at least 60 minutes before reporting / gate closing time so frisking and document verification happen smoothly.

Check Discrepancies Immediately: If you notice any spelling mistake, blurred photo/signature, or wrong detail on your admit card, contact NLU Delhi support immediately via the official portal well before exam day.

PwD / Scribe Guidelines: Specially Abled (PwD) candidates requiring a scribe must ensure their required documents and scribe details are uploaded through their portal account as per NLU Delhi's official PwD guidelines.

Prohibited Items Inside Examination Hall:
  • Smartphones, smartwatches, fitness bands, or Bluetooth earphones.
  • Calculators, digital timers, pen drives, or any electronic gadgets.
  • Bags, wallets, study material, textbooks, or loose paper sheets.
Quick Tip for Aspirants (CLAT + AILET Combo)

Since CLAT 2027 is on 6 December 2026 and AILET 2027 is just one week later on 13 December 2026, make sure you download both admit cards before 6 December (1:30 PM) and keep your focus sharp on Reading Comprehension, Logical Reasoning, and Current Affairs. Check our CLAT 2027 Admit Card & Guidelines guide for your CLAT checklist.

7. Quick Reference: AILET 2027 Exam Pattern (B.A. LL.B.)

While revising in your final days, keep the AILET 2027 offline OMR pattern and negative marking structure in mind:

Section Questions Marks
Section A: English Language 50 Questions 50 Marks
Section B: Current Affairs & General Knowledge 30 Questions 30 Marks
Section C: Logical Reasoning 70 Questions 70 Marks
Total (120 Minutes • -0.25 Negative Marking) 150 Questions 150 Marks
