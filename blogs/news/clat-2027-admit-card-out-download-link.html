CLAT 2027 Admit Card Out: Download Link & Exam Guidelines

Consortium of NLUs has officially published Admit Cards for CLAT 2027 (UG & PG). Access the direct candidate download portal, step-by-step login instructions, mandatory documents list, and essential exam day guidelines.

1. Exam Highlights

Before accessing the portal, review the verified official parameters of the CLAT 2027 examination announced by the Consortium of NLUs:

Particulars Official Details
Exam Name Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) 2027
Conducting Authority Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs)
Programs Covered 5-Year Integrated LLB (UG) & 1-Year LLM (PG)
Admit Card Status Released
Official Website consortiumofnlus.ac.in
Credentials Required Registered Mobile Number & Password
Exam Mode Offline (Pen & Paper-based Test)

2. How to Download CLAT 2027 Admit Card

Follow these simple steps on the official Consortium portal to download and print your Admit Card:

1
Open the Official Portal: Go to consortiumofnlus.ac.in in Chrome or Firefox.
2
Select CLAT 2027 Tab: Click on the active "CLAT 2027" portal link on the top right.
3
Enter Candidate Login: Input your Registered Mobile Number and Password created during registration.
4
Click Login: Open your candidate dashboard and verify your personal details.
5
Download Admit Card PDF: Click on "Download Admit Card" and save the PDF file to your storage.
6
Print Hard Copies: Take at least two clear color printouts on clean A4 white paper.

3. What If You Forgot Your Password?

If you cannot recall your password, reset it easily in three steps:

Click on "Forgot Password?" located beneath the login box.

Enter your registered mobile number and click "Request OTP".

Enter the SMS verification code, choose a new password, and log in to download your Admit Card.

4. Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

Verify these details immediately after downloading your Admit Card:

Candidate's Full Name
Father's & Mother's Name
Application & Roll Number
Date of Birth & Category
Program Applied (UG or PG)
Photograph & Signature Clarity
Exam Center Name & Full Address
Reporting & Gate Closing Timings

5. Mandatory Documents for Exam Day

Security personnel verify your physical documents at the entrance. Ensure you carry all of the following:

Printed Copy of Admit Card (A4 Color)
Valid Original Govt Photo ID Proof
Two Ballpoint Pens (Black or Blue)
2 Recent Passport-Size Photographs
Transparent Water Bottle (Label-Free)

6. Prohibited Items Inside Examination Hall

Consortium centers operate strict zero-tolerance policies. Carrying any banned item leads to immediate cancellation of candidature:

Strictly Banned Items:
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, fitness bands, or bluetooth earphones.
  • Calculators, digital timers, pen drives, or electronic chips.
  • Bags, wallets, notes, textbooks, or loose sheets.
