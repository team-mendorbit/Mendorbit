3. How to Download CLAT 2026 Admit Card Step-by-Step

When thousands of candidates log in at the same moment, official portals can experience brief lag. Follow these exact steps to ensure a smooth download process:

1

Visit the Official Portal

Open your web browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox is highly recommended) and navigate to consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

2

Click on the "CLAT 2026" Tab

On the homepage header or right navigation banner, click the active "CLAT 2026" tab to enter the candidate session area.

3

Enter Your Login Credentials

In the candidate login box located on the right side, carefully enter your Registered 10-digit Mobile Number and the Password you created at registration.

4

Open Candidate Dashboard

Double-check your credentials and click the "Login" button. Your individualized applicant dashboard will appear.

5

Locate & Click "Download Admit Card"

Look at the top bar or next to your application status for the blinking "Download Admit Card" button. Click it to generate your PDF hall ticket.

6

Save PDF & Take Clear Color Printouts

Save the PDF securely on your drive. Print at least 2 to 3 color copies on clean A4 white paper so your photograph, barcode, and signature remain sharp and scannable by exam invigilators.

Forgot Your CLAT Login Password? Here is the Instant Fix

If you have misplaced your password, do not worry—recovering your account takes less than 60 seconds:

  1. On the candidate login box, click on the "Forgot Password?" link.
  2. Type in your 10-digit registered mobile number and click "Request OTP".
  3. Enter the One-Time Password received on your SMS, specify a new secure password, and confirm.
  4. Log in again with your updated credentials to access and print your admit card immediately.

6. Mandatory Documents to Carry on Exam Day

Pack your transparent exam kit the evening before the test. Security at all NLU centers strictly checks the following original proofs:

  • 1. Clear Printed Admit Card: A clean, smudge-free color printout on A4 white paper. Keep an extra backup printout in your folder.
  • 2. Original Government Photo ID Proof (Any One): Aadhaar Card / E-Aadhaar with clear photo, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or verified School/College Photo ID card. (Note: Photocopies or photos on phone are not accepted under any circumstances).
  • 3. Passport-Size Photographs: At least 2 recent color photographs (matching the photograph uploaded on your application form).
  • 4. Stationery: 2 good quality Black or Blue Ballpoint Pens with a transparent body for darkening OMR sheet bubbles. Gel pens and fountain pens are prohibited as they smudge.
  • 5. Transparent Water Bottle: A clear, label-free 500ml water bottle is permitted.
  • 6. PwD / Scribe Certificate (If Applicable): Candidates requiring compensatory time or scribe support must carry their original disability certificate and official Consortium approval form.

7. Prohibited Items & Crucial Exam Hall Guidelines

Strictly Banned Items Inside the Examination Hall

Do not bring mobile phones, smartwatches, digital calculators, Bluetooth earphones, fitness bands, revision notes, bags, or metallic items. Most test centers do not provide locker storage, and security guards will not take responsibility for personal belongings.

Arrive 90 Minutes Early

Center gates strictly close at 1:45 PM. No candidate will be admitted once the gates are shut, regardless of traffic reasons.

Careful OMR Bubble Darkening

Carefully shade your Roll Number and Booklet Code. Use of correction fluid, whitener, or blade on the OMR sheet results in disqualification.

Official Consortium of NLUs Helpdesk & Grievance Redressal

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Is the CLAT 2026 Admit Card released offline via post?

No. The Consortium of NLUs issues admit cards solely through its official portal (consortiumofnlus.ac.in). No physical admit cards are sent via postal dispatch.

2. Can I show a digital copy of my admit card on my phone?

No. Showing digital copies or screenshots on smartphones or smartwatches is completely prohibited. Security guards will only allow candidates holding an authentic printed copy on paper.

3. What should I do if my name or photo is misprinted?

If there is any discrepancy in your name, photograph, category, or signature, write immediately to clat@consortiumofnlus.ac.in with your application number and supporting documents before the examination date.

4. Can I request a change in my allotted examination center?

No. The Consortium strictly does not entertain any request for changing the allotted test center or examination city once the hall ticket has been generated.
