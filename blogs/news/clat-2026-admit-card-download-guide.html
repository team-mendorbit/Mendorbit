The wait is finally over for thousands of law aspirants across India. The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) has officially published the CLAT 2026 Admit Card for both undergraduate (5-Year Integrated LLB) and postgraduate (1-Year LLM) candidates. Below you will find the verified direct download link, step-by-step candidate portal guide, mandatory identity documents checklist, and strict examination day instructions.

3. How to Download CLAT 2026 Admit Card Step-by-Step

When thousands of candidates log in simultaneously, official servers can experience temporary traffic spikes. Follow this exact verified procedure to access your hall ticket smoothly:

1

Visit the Official Portal

Open your web browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox is recommended) and navigate to consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

2

Open the CLAT 2026 Portal

On the homepage, locate and click on the "CLAT 2026" tab situated at the top right of the navigation header.

3

Enter Your Login Credentials

In the candidate login dialog box, enter your Registered Mobile Number and the Password generated during your registration.

4

Access Candidate Dashboard

Click the "Login" button to open your personalized admission portal.

5

Download Hall Ticket PDF

Click the highlighted "Download Admit Card" button. Your hall ticket will open on screen; click the Download icon to save the official PDF file on your device.

6

Print Clear Hard Copies

Print at least two clear copies on standard A4 white paper (color printout is strongly recommended so your photograph, barcode, and signature are sharp and distinct).

4. What If You Forgot Your CLAT Password?

Do not panic if you cannot recall your registration password. The Consortium provides an instant OTP recovery mechanism:

Navigate to the login dialog on consortiumofnlus.ac.in and click the "Forgot Password?" link below the submit button.

Enter your 10-digit registered mobile number and click "Request OTP".

Input the 6-digit OTP received via SMS, set a strong new password, and re-login to download your admit card.

6. Mandatory Documents to Carry to the CLAT Center

Pack your exam kit the evening before the exam. Security personnel will inspect these items before allowing you past the gate:

Printed Copy of CLAT 2026 Admit Card:

Ensure all pages and instructions are printed clearly on clean A4 paper without any ink smudges.

Valid Government-Issued Original Photo ID Proof:

Must be an original physical ID. Acceptable IDs include: Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or valid School/College Photo ID Card. (Digital photos or photocopies are invalid).

Two Ballpoint Pens (Black or Blue):

Pens with transparent bodies are preferred for marking the OMR response sheet. Gel pens, fountain pens, or pencils are strictly prohibited.

Two Recent Passport-Size Photographs:

Carry 2 recent color photographs identical to the one submitted in your online application as a safety backup.

Transparent Water Bottle:

A clear, label-free 500ml water bottle is permitted inside the test hall.

7. Prohibited Items & Exam Center Protocol

Consortium centers enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against unfair means. Possession of any banned item inside the examination hall leads to immediate disqualification:

Strictly Prohibited Inside the Hall:
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, or digital electronic bands.
  • Bluetooth devices, wireless earbuds, microphones, or headphones.
  • Calculators, digital timers, pen drives, or electronic chips.
  • Bags, wallets, pouches, notes, books, or loose scratch paper.
  • Jewelry containing metal or electronic sensors.
