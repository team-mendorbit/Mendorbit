SSC CGL 2026 Official Calendar & Revised Exam Pattern Notification

Staff Selection Commission releases updated tentative exam schedule, post-wise vacancy matrix, and revised Tier-1 normalization rules.

Official Commission Bulletin Alert

Notification issued under the revised SSC computer-based testing schedule.

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially updated the tentative exam dates, application windows, and syllabus guidelines for the Combined Graduate Level Examination (CGL 2026). Over 14,000 Group B & C vacancies are estimated across Central Government Ministries.

1. Important Exam Dates & Milestones

Stage / EventTentative TimelineMode
Online Application FormMay - June 2026Online (ssc.gov.in)
Tier-1 CBT ExaminationSeptember 2026Computer Based Test
Tier-2 Mains ExaminationDecember 2026CBT + Typing Test

2. Tier-1 Examination Pattern Breakdown

Tier-1 consists of 100 objective questions (200 marks total) with a negative marking of 0.50 marks per incorrect attempt:

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: 25 Questions (50 Marks)
  • General Awareness: 25 Questions (50 Marks)
  • Quantitative Aptitude: 25 Questions (50 Marks)
  • English Comprehension: 25 Questions (50 Marks)
