Staff Selection Commission releases updated tentative exam schedule, post-wise vacancy matrix, and revised Tier-1 normalization rules.

3. Tier-1 Examination Pattern & Sectional Weightage

Tier-1 is purely qualifying in nature; marks are not counted toward final merit ranking, but clearing the normalized category cutoff is mandatory to qualify for Tier-2:

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: 25 Questions (50 Marks) — High-scoring zone; focus on Syllogisms, Series, and Non-verbal analogies.
  • General Awareness (GK/GS): 25 Questions (50 Marks) — Modern History, Indian Polity, Science & Last 6 Months Current Affairs.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: 25 Questions (50 Marks) — Heavy focus on Advance Maths (Geometry, Trigonometry, Algebra, Mensuration).
  • English Comprehension: 25 Questions (50 Marks) — Reading Comprehension, Vocab (Idioms, OWS), and Error Spotting.
  • Total: 100 Questions | 200 Marks | Duration: 60 Minutes (Negative marking: 0.50 marks per error).

4. Tier-2 Mains Exam: Merit Determining Structure

Final selection and ministry allocation are determined solely on the basis of aggregate marks scored in Paper-I of Tier-2 (Total: 390 Marks):

Section I: Mathematical Abilities & Reasoning (180 Marks)

30 Maths questions + 30 Reasoning questions (3 marks each). 1 hour allotted. Negative marking is 1 mark per wrong attempt.

Section II: English Language & General Awareness (210 Marks)

45 English questions (135 Marks) + 25 GA questions (75 Marks). This section makes up the largest individual chunk of merit marks.

Section III: Qualifying Computer Module & DEST Typing Test

20 Computer Knowledge questions (60 Marks - Qualifying) + 15-Minute Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) typing ~2000 key depressions.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (SSC CGL FAQs)

Q: What is the minimum educational qualification for SSC CGL?

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized University in India. Final-year students are also eligible provided their degree results are published before the specified cutoff date.

Q: What is a safe raw score in Tier-1 for General category candidates?

Targeting a raw score of 145+ out of 200 marks consistently across mock tests provides a comfortable safety margin regardless of shift difficulty variance.
