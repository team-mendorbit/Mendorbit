Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for choosing Mendorbit (https://mendorbit.in). We take utmost pride in delivering quality conceptual education and dedicated student mentorship. Please read this policy carefully before enrolling in any paid batch, course, or test series.

1. Free Trial & Transparent Demos

We provide extensive free full-length conceptual lectures on our official YouTube channel (@mendorbit), demo PDFs, and notes before enrolment so students and parents can evaluate the quality of our teaching and mentorship methodology firsthand.

2. Digital Content & Batch Access Policy

Due to the instant delivery nature of digital educational materials (such as live class credentials, recorded video archives, downloadable DPPs, test series, and proprietary lecture notes), all course and batch purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been activated on the student learning portal (app.mendorbit.in).

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

A full or partial refund will be granted in the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate Transaction: If a student is charged multiple times due to technical gateway timeouts or network latency, the duplicate amount will be refunded 100% within 5–7 working days.
  • Batch Cancellation: In the rare event that Mendorbit cancels a planned batch before commencement, students will receive an immediate 100% refund or an option to migrate to an equivalent batch.
  • Technical Provisioning Failure: If technical errors on our server prevent you from accessing the batch for more than 7 consecutive days without resolution from our engineering team.

4. How to Request a Refund

If your request meets any of the eligible criteria listed above, please email us within 48 hours of the transaction:

Email: support@mendorbit.in

Subject Line: Refund Request - [Transaction ID] - [Student Name]

Required Details: Payment Receipt, Registered Phone Number, Batch Name, and Bank Transaction Reference (UTR).

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment source (UPI, Debit Card, Net Banking, or Wallet) within 5 to 7 business days in compliance with standard Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment settlement guidelines.

6. Contact Support

For any doubts regarding payments, enrolment, or technical access, reach out directly via our Contact Support page or email contact@mendorbit.in.
