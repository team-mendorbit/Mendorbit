The 4 Pillars of Our Mission

How we empower ordinary, overlooked students to defeat self-doubt, overcome digital distraction, and conquer board and competitive exams.

1. Zero-Ground Conceptual Rebuilding

Most educators assume you already know the basics and rush through the syllabus. At Mendorbit, we begin from ground zero. We explain why a formula exists, not just how to cram it. If you score 50% today, we teach with the exact patience required to lift you to 95%+.

  • First-principles conceptual clarity
  • Line-by-line NCERT mastery
  • Safe environment to ask basic doubts without judgment

2. The Distraction-Free Virtual Room

Changing your physical room is easy; escaping the toxic digital chaos on your screen is hard. We have engineered Mendorbit as a quiet, distraction-free virtual room. No clickbait, no dopamine algorithms, no algorithmic shorts — only deep, uninterrupted focus.

  • Zero pop-up recommendations or side-video rabbit holes
  • Structured timelines and study timers
  • Protects your mental stamina for long study sessions

3. ₹15,000 Quality Without Paywalls

Free should never mean inferior. Our free batches follow the rigorous schedule, DPPs (Daily Practice Problems), chapter-wise handwritten notes, and regular test series that corporate coachings lock behind expensive ₹15,000 subscriptions.

  • Daily Practice Problems (DPPs) with solutions
  • High-definition handwritten topper revision notes
  • Accessible to every village and pin-code in India

4. The "Bade Bhaiya / Badi Didi" Mentorship

Founded by brothers and sister — Satyam Thakur (IIT Patna student), Shivam Thakur, and Sundram Thakur (B.Tech CSE). We are students and recent learners who know the pain, pressure, and mental fatigue you face every day. We mentor you like elder siblings.

  • Learning from real student mistakes without shame
  • Human connection over commercial transactions
  • Standing with you until your goal is won
